# Antisy

Swiss bot protection for website forms and logins. Hosted at Infomaniak, in Geneva. Nothing for your visitors to solve.

One tag in the page, one call from your server, one verdict: `allow` or `deny`.

- Documentation: https://www.antisy.com/en/documentation/verification
- Integration guides: https://www.antisy.com/en/guides
